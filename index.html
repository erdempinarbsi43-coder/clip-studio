Clip Studio

Clip Studio is the private publishing tool of a small group of short-form video pages run by one owner: We Were In Chat (stream moments), Two Hour Mark (podcast moments) and Day 90 Club (training and glow-up moments).

The tool prepares short vertical clips on the owner's own computer from source material that the rights holders provide for clipping. With the owner's permission it sends finished clips, with their captions, to the owner's own accounts through the official TikTok Content Posting API, the Instagram API and the YouTube Data API, and reads the posts' basic statistics.

The tool is used only by the account owner for these accounts. It is not offered to other users and no accounts can be created for it.
